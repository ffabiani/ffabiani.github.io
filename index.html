I am an Associate Professor in the DYSCO Research Unit at the IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca (IT), joined in 2022 as an Assistant Professor. I received the B.Sc. degree in Bioengineering, the M.Sc. degree in Control Engineering, and the Ph.D. degree (cum laude) in Control Engineering, all from the University of Pisa (IT), in 2012, 2015, and 2019 respectively. In 2017-2018, I visited the Delft Center for Systems and Control at TU Delft (NL), where in 2018-2019 I spent one year as post-doctoral Research Fellow. In 2019-2022 I was Post-Doctoral Research Assistant in the Control Group at the University of Oxford (UK).

Since 2025, I am an Associate Editor for the IEEE L-CSS. I was also a Conference Editorial Board for EUCA and L4DC.

My research combines robust control, system identification, machine learning and optimization, and concentrates on developing data-driven theoretical tools and algorithmic methods for learning models and computing control decisions with explicit guarantees. Applications span from optimal control of multi-agent and uncertain systems, such as power and traffic network, to emerging control applications in smart grids and smart cities.
