Since May 2026, I am an Associate Professor in the DYSCO Research Unit at the IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca (IT), joined in 2022 as an Assistant Professor. I received the B.Sc. degree in Bioengineering, the M.Sc. degree in Control Engineering, and the Ph.D. degree (cum laude) in Control Engineering, all from the University of Pisa (IT), in 2012, 2015, and 2019 respectively. In 2017-2018, I visited the Delft Center for Systems and Control at TU Delft (NL) , where in 2018-2019 I spent one year as post-doctoral Research Fellow. Successively, in 2019-2022 I was Post-Doctoral Research Assistant in the Control Group at the University of Oxford (UK).

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, game theory and control engineering, and concentrates on developing both data-driven theoretical tools and algorithmic methods for modern control design. These include optimal control of multi-agent and uncertain systems such as power and traffic network, and emerging control applications in smart grids and smart cities.
