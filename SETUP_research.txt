FIGURE EXAMPLES

Plant certificates (learning.svg): a rotated ellipse illustrates directional
plant-parameter uncertainty. A linear image of sampled points on that ellipse
produces a synthetic family of stable, nonnormal closed-loop matrices. The
induced matrix 2-norms of their powers illustrate transient amplification and
model-to-model variation. This is a conceptual illustration of the certification
question, not a confidence set estimated from experimental data or a proof of a
uniform certificate for a physical plant.

Decision generalization (uncertainty.svg): quadratic objective contours show an
underlying optimum and an empirical optimum. Gradient descent is stopped after
five iterations, separating the returned iterate's numerical error from the
statistical shift in the optimum. A separate schematic plot shows a decaying
numerical term, a growing sensitivity-related statistical term, and their sum,
for a fixed sample size. These curves illustrate a possible trade-off under
appropriate assumptions; they are not a measured convergence result or a
universal finite-sample bound.

Games (games.svg): the two-agent example has x1 >= 0, x2 >= 0, x1+x2 <= 1,
J1=(x1-0.22-0.35*x2)^2, and J2=(x2-0.16-0.25*x1)^2. Constrained best-response
updates from (0.76,0.10) approach the intersection of the best-response curves.
The accompanying network illustrates distributed decision-making.
